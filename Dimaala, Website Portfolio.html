About me

Hi! I'm Mariane, a student who fell in love with programming. I've taken Computer Programming 1 & 2 (Python and Java), Discrete Math, HCI 1 & 2 (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), OOP 1, Data Structures & Algorithms, and Intro to Computing (C++).

I enjoy building small projects to practice what I learn — whether it's a simple game in Python, a Java console app, or a website like this one. One of my favorite projects was being the Game Designer for the DWCC Memory Game, a school project where I helped design the levels, rules, and overall player experience.

I'm also a proud member of the Junior Philippine Computer Society (JPCS) – DWCC Chapter, where I've joined several IT webinars on topics like ethical hacking, mobile robotics, AI, and cybersecurity. These talks keep me inspired and remind me why I love tech.

When I'm not studying, I like to sketch, read, and tinker with code just for fun. I'm still a student, but I'm proud of how far I've come!
